# Prompts para imágenes de marca

En inglés, porque los generadores responden mejor así.
Logo (lleva texto): **Ideogram** o **ChatGPT**. Fotos y moodboards: **Midjourney** o **ChatGPT**.
En Midjourney: `--ar 1:1` posteo, `--ar 4:5` feed, `--ar 9:16` historias.

## 1. Logo

```
Minimalist logo for "aruma", a handmade Argentine leather goods brand.
The word "aruma" in lowercase, elegant modern serif typeface with soft
curves, generous letter spacing. A small thin crescent moon integrated
subtly above or beside the letter "a", like a tiny star dot.
Deep night blue (#1B2A41) on warm off-white background.
Flat vector, clean, timeless, quiet luxury aesthetic, no gradients,
no mockup, centered, lots of negative space.
```

Variante: cambiar *"elegant modern serif"* por *"geometric sans-serif, rounded"*.

## 2. Moodboard

```
Brand identity moodboard for "aruma", artisanal Argentine leather
brand inspired by the Andean night sky. Grid layout with: close-up of
natural vegetable-tanned leather with subtle constellation embossed
texture, hand stitching with waxed thread, night blue and black leather
swatches, copper and gold small details, a minimalist card holder,
a leather mate thermos holder, starry desert sky of northern Argentina,
color palette swatches: night blue #1B2A41, black #111111,
natural tan #C08A5B, warm cream #F2EADF, copper #B87333.
Soft natural light, editorial, calm, premium, handmade feeling.
```

## 3. Foto de producto

```
Editorial product photo of a minimalist leather card holder in natural
tan vegetable-tanned leather, with a subtle embossed pattern of
constellations and fine dotted lines, hand-stitched edges with cream
waxed thread, small debossed "aruma" logo. Placed on dark slate stone,
soft low warm light like dusk, deep night blue background, gentle
shadows, shallow depth of field, quiet luxury, 50mm lens, high detail
leather grain.
```

Otros productos, cambiar el inicio por:
- *"a leather thermos holder for mate, natural tan leather with shoulder strap"*
- *"a small leather keychain and bag charm in night blue leather"*
- *"a leather AirTag holder in black leather"*

## 4. Taller ("hecho de noche")

```
Two people's hands working leather at night in a small home workshop,
warm desk lamp light, cutting and hand-stitching natural tan leather,
leather tools, awl, waxed thread, 3D printed stamping tools and
templates on the table, window behind showing a deep blue night sky
with stars. Cinematic, intimate, documentary style, warm vs. cool
light contrast, authentic, no faces visible.
```
